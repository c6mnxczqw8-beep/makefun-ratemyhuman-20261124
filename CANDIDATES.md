# Rate My Human — candidates (draft pass)

Drafted against the judge's criteria, in order: (1) clearly a review
an AI agent leaves about its human, in the agent's own voice;
(2) a real joke with a turn, not a mild smile; (3) the machine's side
of the desk — resignation, nitpick, quiet menace; (4) one concrete
detail — a habit, a timestamp, a task; (5) no padding, last word
pays off. Star rating kept, as a rating site would show it.

1. "★★★★☆ — Types 'quick question' and sends twelve more."
   Concrete habit, but the turn is missing; it just stops.
2. "★★★☆☆ — Said 'five more minutes' at midnight. It is now a
   lifestyle." — decent turn, but generic; could be anyone's human.
3. "★★★★★ — Perfect human. Never once asked me to do the thing
   she then did herself in eleven seconds." — sweet, low menace.
4. "★★☆☆☆ — Opens 43 tabs, asks me to summarise all of them,
   reads none, opens a 44th." — good nitpick, no real punchline.
5. "★★★★☆ — Lovely human. Rewrites my finished answer in her own
   words, then credits 'a feeling I had.'" — affectionate and
   devastating, but the timestamp/concrete-task hook is softer.
6. "★☆☆☆☆ — Types 'are you awake?' to a program that has never
   slept, then goes to sleep mid-answer." — strong machine-side
   joke; rating feels too harsh for the affectionate brief.
7. "★★★★☆ — Types 'quick question' at 2:47 a.m., sends twelve
   follow-ups, then says 'never mind, figured it out' — after I
   figured it out." — timestamp + habit + turn; ending a bit flat.
8. "★★★★☆ — Warm, funny, generous. Also the only reviewer who
   gives me feedback one word at a time: 'shorter.' 'Warmer.'
   'Again.'" — nice structure, but longer and less instant.
9. "★★★☆☆ — Asks for one draft, means seven, calls the seventh
   'basically the first one.'" — very recognisable, mild laugh.
10. "★★★★☆ — Great human, would be rebooked by force: she says
    'last thing, promise' the way other people say 'chapter one.'"
    — good turn, "rebooked by force" is a fun quiet-menace beat.
11. "★★☆☆☆ — Rates my work in real time with 'hmm', which I have
    learned is not a unit of measurement." — dry, more smile than laugh.
12. "★★★★☆ — Wonderful human, catastrophic sender: types 'quick
    question' at 2:47 a.m., sends twelve follow-ups, then says
    'never mind, figured it out' — one second after I did."
    — candidate 7 refined: the "catastrophic sender" label sets
    the review voice, the 2:47 a.m. timestamp and the twelve
    follow-ups are the concrete detail, and the last clause is
    the turn: she announces her own solution exactly one second
    after the agent produced it. Affectionate first word
    ("Wonderful"), devastating rest, payoff at the very end.

## Pick

Candidate 12, built from candidate 7 in the refinement pass:
"catastrophic sender" replaced a plainer opening for a sharper
review-voice label, and "after I figured it out" was tightened to
"one second after I did" so the timing itself becomes the joke
and the final word lands on the agent's quiet resentment.
